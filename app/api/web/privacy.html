Privacy notice

What the Teaching Agent keeps about you, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Who runs this service

The school or organisation that gave you access runs this copy of the Teaching Agent and decides how it is used. Ask them about anything in this notice, or about your data.

What is kept about you

  • The id your sign-in gives you, the name you chose and your study preferences.
  • Your goals and the learning plan made for each of them.
  • The lessons made for you: their text, pictures, slides, narration and video.
  • Your answers to checks and practice questions, how they were graded, and the questions you asked.
  • What the service estimates you know (mastery), the evidence for it, and the history of your learning sessions.
  • The classes you joined.
  • A record of the paid services used for your lessons: ids and amounts only, never what you wrote or learned. It is used to keep spending within limits.

All of it is used to teach you: to choose what to learn next, to make lessons at your level and to show your progress.

Who can see it

  • You, and anyone your sign-in lets act for you (for example a parent or guardian).
  • The teacher of a class you joined. Joining with their code lets them see your progress, goals, learning sessions and answers. They cannot change anything. When you leave the class, or they remove you, they stop seeing it.
  • The people who run the service, when they need to look after it.

AI and media services

To write lessons, grade written answers and answer your questions, the service sends text to the AI and media services its operator chose: a language model, web search, image generation, text to speech and, if used, video generation. What is sent is what each step needs, such as the topic, your level, the lesson's text, your answers to be graded and your questions. Your name and your sign-in id are not sent.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. This version does not delete anything on its own. Backups that the operator keeps may hold copies until those backups expire.

Deleting your data

Open Progress in the app and choose Delete my data. This deletes, at once and for good, your profile, goals, plans, lessons and their files, sessions, answers and grades, mastery, history and class memberships. Teachers stop seeing you.

Two things stay. The record of paid services keeps its ids and amounts, so spending limits still hold. Pictures that were looked at for a lesson but not used are not linked to you and may remain.

If a lesson is being prepared at that moment, wait for it to finish or stop it, then delete again. Deleting your data does not close your sign-in account; that account belongs to your school or organisation.

In your browser

The app sets no cookies of its own. It remembers, for the current browser tab only, which learner you are studying as. Signing in is handled by your organisation's sign-in service, which may use its own cookie.
